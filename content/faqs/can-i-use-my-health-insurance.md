question: Can I use my health insurance?
answer: >-
  I do not participate in insurance panels for both philosophical and ethical
  reasons. This allows me to protect your privacy, maintain the freedom to
  tailor therapy to your unique needs rather than an insurance company's
  requirements, and preserve the independence to practice in a way that aligns
  with my clinical values.


  I intentionally maintain a small caseload so I can devote significant time outside of sessions to studying the philosophical and clinical concerns most relevant to my clients. I also invest in ongoing consultation, advanced training, and voluntary existential supervision despite already being independently licensed. This behind-the-scenes work is an important part of the care I provide, but it is largely unsupported by the insurance model.


  If your plan offers out-of-network benefits, I'm happy to provide a superbill for possible reimbursement.
