question: What the heck is "existential psychotherapy"?!
answer: >-
  Think of it as therapy for the bigger questions. Existential psychotherapy
  grew out of a rich philosophical tradition that stretches from Socrates
  through thinkers like Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Heidegger, and Viktor Frankl.
  Rather than viewing emotional suffering as something to simply eliminate,
  these thinkers recognized that many of life's struggles arise from being
  human: facing uncertainty, making difficult choices, navigating relationships,
  and searching for meaning.


  Yes, we'll work with anxiety, depression, trauma, and relationships, but we'll also explore purpose, identity, freedom, responsibility, and what it means to live a life that genuinely feels like your own. My goal isn't simply to help you feel better. It's to help you understand yourself more deeply so you can live more deliberately.
